01 / COGNITION & AGING

HONOURS THESIS · 2023–2024

Cardiovascular risk
& cognitive aging

Exploring the relationship between cardiovascular risk factors and cognitive profiles through the Kaplan–Baycrest Neurocognitive Assessment.

NeuropsychologyData analysisjamovi
Inside the project +

Under the supervision of Dr. Guy Proulx, I reviewed 97 patient records and extracted demographic, clinical, and cognitive assessment data.

  • Used k-means clustering to explore cognitive profiles.
  • Conducted chi-square tests, Welch’s ANOVAs, and effect-size comparisons in jamovi.
  • Produced a thesis connecting the analysis to cognitive aging and assessment.

Role: honours thesis student · York University
